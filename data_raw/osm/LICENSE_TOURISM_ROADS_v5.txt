The data is a community map snapshot. Tourism classification, opening, price,
popularity, actual entrance, current access and traffic are not verified.
Road geometry/topology is not a complete legal navigation engine. Turn
restriction relations and real-time closures were not acquired.
Queries use a fixed Jeju bounding box and never contain a user's GPS position.
